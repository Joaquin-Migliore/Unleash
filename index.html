The feeling
Before we start

Let's be honest for a second.

All of this landed on HR this year. And somewhere in the noise, you're supposed to feel on top of it.
Then you open LinkedIn

It feels like everyone has already automated their entire company, built 12 agents, and somehow trained their executive team on AI before breakfast.

So, are you already behind?

Feeling behind is normal.

People who hadn't touched Claude two weeks ago just found how fast you can actually build, and they're sharing amazing things online. It makes the gap feel far larger than it really is.

But staying frozen is optional.

You beat AI paralysis by building one small, useful thing. Then you grow from there, quicker than you'd think.

AI transformation is a confidence-building journey.

And confidence surfaces, organically, from a safe and inspired environment.

Some context on who we are
Superside is the world's leading AI-first creative partner.

We help in-house teams at brands like Intuit, Amazon, DoorDash, Figma, and Reddit scale on-brand creative that delivers, pairing world-class human talent with AI-first workflows and a Brand Brain that gets smarter with every project.

But how did we go from AI in creative is a neat niche experiment to having most of our creatives become AI certified, running genuinely complex workflows and making mind-bending work for clients?

How we enabled our creatives
We built the muscle three ways. It took about a year and a half.
CAISU
Creative AI Skill Unlock
A quick assessment every creative hire has to pass before working on AI projects. The baseline that they can use AI tools properly before touching a brief.
AI, Real
The bootcamp
A 4-week bootcamp on real client briefs where creatives build AI skills through deliberate practice, guided by experienced Creative AI facilitators.
In the real work
Peer learning
AI embedded in live projects and peer learning, not a training module off to the side.
Talent and performance

We backed it with honest talent decisions.

Talent reviews between cycles to see who needed support.
Real investment in upskilling for those ready to grow.
And honest calls where the growth did not come.

Then we hired for AI curiosity, adaptability, and product-mindedness, which, together with enablement, kept building our AI talent density and momentum toward AI-native creatives.

Traditional HR work you know well, pointed at one long-term goal: AI talent density.

The right culture and surroundings inspire everyone around them.

A can-do attitude toward what's newly possible, the speed of change, and constant woah moments about what AI can do. That environment made rethinking how we work in HR feel natural, even without building complex creative workflows ourselves.

03
Zoom in: AI HR experiments
Inspired by what the company was doing, we broke our own AI paralysis and started building, one small experiment at a time, each one to iterate on and grow if it proved successful.
Before any build

Stay solution agnostic.

Focus on the problem.

1Spot the areas worth improving, from metrics and feedback.
2Use first-principles thinking to form hypotheses, then test them and pinpoint the core issue through techniques like Socratic questioning or the 5 whys.
3Build minimum lovable products, then iterate, iterate, iterate.
How we ran them

For the AI experiments, we spun up mini-squads.

A lightweight framework, separate from everyone's day-to-day, just for running the experiments.

Squads
Cross-team, project-based squads on top of the day-to-day. Everyone joins one each quarter.
Sprints
Biweekly, shipping minimum lovable products, then iterating on real feedback.
Fail faster
Learning fast beats launching something perfect a quarter too late.

People Experience journey

Giving the whole Supersider experience a single, living map.

Experiment 01 · People Experience journey

An employee isn't buying a perk or a salary. They're subscribing to an experience.

Every month they stay, they renew. Every resignation is a cancellation. That experience is a product, and our job is to grow its lifetime value: help people grow further, stay longer, and ramp faster.

Experiment 01 · People Experience journey

It started with two gaps.

01
No holistic view of the moments that shape the Supersider experience, which made spotting gaps, planning, and improving harder than it should be.
02
Our engagement surveys didn't feel like a holistic capture of the experience, missing the hiring, preboarding, onboarding, and offboarding parts of the journey entirely.
Experiment 01 · People Experience journey

So AI helped us map the entire journey.

We gave Claude deep context on all our people programs and built a skill that had it act as an HR consultant, iterating with us toward a fully representative map, from the moment someone signs their offer to the day they leave.

Journey map · drop journey-map.png in the folder

The Supersider Journey Map: the experience people see on top, the programs and squads that power it beneath.

Experiment 01 · People Experience journey

A static file wouldn't get used. So we toolified it.

With Claude, we turned the map into something leaders actually want to open.

Filter down to specific moments.
Add their own notes.
See where each team member sits in their journey.

And our own People team, non-engineers, vibe-coded the whole thing in plain language, building and iterating fast.

Experiment 01 · People Experience journey

Then we made our people data holistic.

We measure the key moments, sometimes with survey questions, but often through proxies:

Town hall attendance.
PTO and policy usage.
Internal ticketing timings and volume.

AI helped us code Apps Scripts that automatically pull HRIS data and refresh these dashboards, so maintenance is minimal and the team can spend its time digging into the actual trends.

Experiment 01 · People Experience journey

Then we turned the data into stories.

We used Claude to combine our own insights with the patterns it spotted, then to quickly code a customized HTML dashboard for each department, surfacing the trends that matter and turning them into a cohesive story that grabs stakeholders and gets them to sponsor and drive change.
